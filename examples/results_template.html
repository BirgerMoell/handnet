HandNet
neural networks whose weights were written by hand

What happens if you build a neural network the way an engineer builds a circuit — by deciding every weight instead of growing them with gradient descent? Two networks, zero training, every unit named. A text-quality gate that works in every script, and MNIST, the hello world of machine learning, done without the learning.

1 · Text quality in every language

handnet.text_quality.HandNet scores a document 0–100 for pretraining-filter quality: markup, mojibake, repetition, shouting, tables, logs, navigation fragments. Its "tokenizer" is 40 per-character Unicode attributes — letter, case, digit, punctuation class, script group, mojibake markers — so no weight knows any word and the same 627 numbers score Swedish, Georgian, Japanese, Arabic, Hindi or Thai. Downstream: a width-5 convolution with 48 named pattern channels, a ReLU-RNN of run-length counters, pooling, and a four-layer MLP of 46 named penalty/reward detectors with point budgets (-mojibake 35, -markup 20, +sentences 8, …), length gates and script gates.

Ablation: which detectors matter?

{{AB_ROWS}}
variantmean AUCΔworst language

Only the formatting group (line breaks mid-sentence, bullet lines, space before punctuation, ellipses) carries real weight: without it the mean drops from {{AB_FULL}} to 0.858 and Thai collapses to 0.69. The other groups are nearly redundant here, and switching off the rewards or the tokens group slightly improves agreement. An honest negative result for the design method: several detectors that are easy to justify (shouting, digit runs, giant tokens) contribute nothing measurable on this benchmark.

What it cannot do: grammar. On minimal pairs differing by one inflection or a swapped word it is at chance — a character-level net with hand-set weights has no morphology and no word order. It is the cheap first filter.

2 · MNIST without the training

handnet.mnist_holes.MNISTNet classifies digits with five hand-drawn 5×5 kernels (ink, horizontal, vertical, / and \), a recurrent hole finder that floods the background from the border so that whatever is never reached is a loop (the inside of a 0, 6, 8, 9), zone pooling over a 5×5 grid, each digit drawn by hand as 5×5 ASCII-art sketches per channel (a sketch is a prototype: its unit computes W·x − ½‖W‖², minus the squared distance to the drawing), and 29 named evidence units (four:open_top, bottom_bar, hole:top_only, …) that vote.

4 · How this was made

Both networks were written by Claude (Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.1) in conversation with Birger Moëll, who set the tasks, the rule and the standard of evidence. The text net was written first as the cheap pre-filter of a multilingual data-quality pipeline for the OpenEuroLLM languages; this repo lifts it out and adds the MNIST net, the evaluations, the paper and this page, all in one session on 4 November 2026.

The rule. A weight may be set by reasoning ("clean prose has about one sentence end per hundred characters", "a 4 has an empty top centre") and checked against data, but never fit to data: no gradients, no optimiser, no search over weight values. Every non-zero weight is in the source next to the unit it belongs to.

What "checked" meant. Text: a handful of hand-written prose and junk examples in five scripts (now the tests), and reading the worst-scored FineWeb-2 documents to see which detector was wrong. MNIST: class-conditional means of the zone features on 5,000 training images to put each threshold at the knee between a digit and its neighbours; sketches drawn from how the average digit looks at 5×5; about a dozen rounds of "look at the confusion matrix, which unit is to blame, adjust its votes". The test set was scored once.

Why it is a network and not a rule engine. Everything is matrix products, ReLUs and one recurrence; to_torch() returns an nn.Module with the same tensors, and the tests check it matches NumPy to 1e-3. Which also means the obvious next experiment — fine-tune from a designed initialisation — is one line away. This repo deliberately does not run it.
